HKL'S SPORTS-EMERGENCY FOOTBALL MEDICINE WORKSHOP 2026

On-Field Sudden Cardiac Arrest · Participant app

All colours

ORANGE

Airway / C-spine

WHERE YOU STAND

At the player's HEAD (FIFA PEAP orange mark). Never leave the head.

WHAT YOU BRING

LMA (i-gel) and BVM with reservoir.

WHAT YOU DO

  • Airway opening manoeuvres (jaw thrust if experienced). Manual in-line C-spine control if trauma.
  • Insert i-gel / LMA. Attach bag-valve.
  • Squeeze the bag ONCE EVERY 10 COMPRESSIONS.
  • At a shock: LMA connected (closed circuit) — leave the BVM and oxygen connected. Face mask only — lift the BVM off the face, replace it after.
  • Control the log-roll from the HEAD.

WHAT YOU SAY

“Airway open. LMA in.”

“No LMA: OPA in — bagging.”

“Holding the head. Ready to roll.”

“Roll on three. One, two, three, ROLL.”

If it is not your colour, do not reach in.

FIFA: Airway after compressions + AED are running. i-gel LMA: bag once every 10 compressions. Face-mask only, or i-gel leaking: pause for 30:2.

FIFA OFFICIAL ROLE CARD (2025)

The person at the head of the player.

In trauma

  • Immobilise the cervical spine (jaw thrust at the same time if needed).
  • Speak to the player to reassure them and explain what is happening.

In a log roll

  • Take the head.
  • Take the lead: give clear instructions and ask each member to confirm they understood.

In cardiac arrest

  • Initial airway-opening manoeuvres.
  • Insert the LMA and attach the bag/valve.
  • One ventilation every ten compressions with the LMA.
  • Face mask instead of LMA: apply and hold the mask; the compressor gives 2 breaths after every 30.
