If it is not your colour, do not reach in — except Black 1, the team leader, who is in overall control and steps in when a job is not being done.

Numbers for hand signals: Each colour has a number — Black 1, Orange 2, Red 3, Blue 4, White 5, Green 6 — in the order FIFA's Emergency Care Manual lists the team members. When the crowd is too loud to hear, the team leader can call or hand-signal a role by its number.

EXTRICATION TEAM

The current FIFA PEAP shows six roles: Black, Orange, Red, Green, Blue and White. Earlier versions also marked a separate extrication team in yellow, usually four first aiders provided by the stadium. Some venues and teaching centres still use one. If yours does, include them in the pre-match plan: they wait beside the scoop and basket, facing the player, and join only when the team leader calls them — to help with a log roll or to carry.

ABOUT THE APP

A free study guide to pitchside emergency care in football, built around FIFA's medical set piece: the six colour roles, sudden cardiac arrest, the primary survey, cervical spine injury, concussion assessment and the emergency care bag — with team drills in 3D, an AED pads trainer and match-day tools. It summarises FIFA's published guidance in plain language and links to FIFA's own documents and films.
