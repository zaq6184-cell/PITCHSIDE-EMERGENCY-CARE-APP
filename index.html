STRETCHER TEAM

The current FIFA PEAP shows six roles: Black, Orange, Red, Green, Blue and White. Earlier versions also marked a separate stretcher team in yellow, usually four bearers provided by the stadium. Some venues and teaching centres still use one. If yours does, include them in the pre-match plan: they wait beside the scoop and basket, facing the player, and join only when the team leader calls them — to help with a log roll or to carry.

ABOUT THE APP

A free study guide to pitchside emergency care in football, built around FIFA's medical set piece: the six colour roles, sudden cardiac arrest, the primary survey, cervical spine injury, concussion assessment and the emergency care bag. It summarises FIFA's published guidance in plain language and links to FIFA's own documents and films.

Developed by the Emergency and Orthopaedics team of Hospital Kuala Lumpur, in collaboration with the Asian Football Confederation (AFC).
