CASE SIMULATOR

Make the calls, watch the monitor

Three pitchside cases. Every decision costs time on the case clock, and the monitor answers. The debrief shows each decision and what it meant.

Teaching with it: open a case in full screen on a projector and let the group call each decision before you tap it. Sound on gives the monitor's beep and alarms.
