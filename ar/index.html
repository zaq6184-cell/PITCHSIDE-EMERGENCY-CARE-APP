HOW TO USE IT

Google's AR viewer

  1. 1Tap See the team in your room (AR) — or Play the whole drill in AR. Google's AR viewer opens over the app; the first time, allow the camera.
  2. 2Hold the phone at chest height, pointing at the floor, and sweep it slowly from side to side until the team appears.
  3. 3The team stands on the floor at full size, the player's feet towards you, and works through the step again and again. Turn the sound up: each colour's call-outs play in time with the moves.
  4. 4Walk round it: each colour's number is on the floor behind them — Black 1, Orange 2, Red 3, Blue 4, White 5, Green 6.
  5. 5Pinch with two fingers to make it smaller (desk size for a small group), drag with one finger to move it, twist two fingers to turn it.
  6. 6Tap the back arrow (top left) to come back, choose another step and open it the same way.

A phone that cannot do AR opens the team in 3D instead, still moving: drag to turn it, pinch to zoom.

Live AR with steps · Chrome

On phones where Chrome can run AR itself, a second button keeps you in the app: the drill plays live in the room, and you control it.

  1. 1Tap Live AR with steps and allow the camera.
  2. 2Sweep the floor slowly until a gold ring appears, then tap the screen to put the team on it.
  3. 3The step plays with the call-outs and captions. The gold button pauses and plays it; Slow runs it at half speed; Play all goes through every step in turn.
  4. 4‹ › at the top step through the drill; Turn rotates the team; Table top shrinks it for a desk; tap the floor to move it.
  5. 5Tap ✕ to leave AR.

Make a video: record the screen while the team plays — swipe down from the top twice and tap Screen record, with media sound on so the call-outs are on it.

BEFORE YOU START

  • Space: about 4 × 5 m of clear floor for full size — or shrink the team to fit a desk.
  • Light: a well-lit room. In the dark the phone cannot see the floor.
  • Floor: matt, with some pattern — carpet, tiles, wood. Plain white, black or shiny floors are hard to follow.
  • Sound: volume up, silent mode off. On Android the call-outs play with the moving team; AR Quick Look on iPhone shows the moves without sound.
  • Internet: each step downloads once — up to about 1.3 MB on Android and 3 MB on iPhone; the whole drill about 2.5 MB on Android and 5 MB on iPhone.
  • Android: the guide open in Chrome, with the Google app and Google Play Services for AR. iPhone and iPad: Safari, with iOS up to date.
  • Safety: keep the floor clear, and look up from the screen when you walk.

IF IT DOES NOT WORK

The button does nothing, or opens a web page+

Open the guide in Chrome — not inside WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook (use ⋮ → Open in Chrome). Update the Google app, and install or update Google Play Services for AR.

It shows the team in 3D, but no AR+

The phone may not support Google's AR — check Google's list of phones. You can still turn and zoom the team in 3D, or use the 3D drills in the app.

The team never appears+

Turn on more light, move the phone more slowly, aim at a floor with some pattern, and step back so more of it is in view.

The team does not move+

Start is the team in place, standing still: choose a step or the whole drill to see them work. If a step stays still, update the Google app (Android) or iOS (iPhone).

There is no sound+

Turn the volume up and the silent switch off. The call-outs play on Android, in Google's AR viewer and in Live AR. AR Quick Look on iPhone plays no sound: the 3D drills in the app have the voices.

It is too big for the room+

Pinch to shrink it, or turn it so the player lies along the longest side of the room.

The team drifts or floats+

Sweep the floor a little longer before it appears, and avoid shiny floors and people walking through the view.

Nothing happens on an iPhone+

Use Safari: other browsers on iPhone may not open AR Quick Look.

On a laptop or desktop+

AR needs a phone or tablet camera: scan the QR code by the step with your phone.

TEACHING WITH AR

  • Full size on the floor: each trainee kneels at their colour's numbered mark, beside their AR colour, and you step through the drill together.
  • Watch, then do: play a step in AR, then the group runs the same step on the floor beside the AR team.
  • Step by step: open step 1, then ask what each colour does next before you open step 2. In Live AR, pause on a moment to ask, and use Slow to show each move at half speed.
  • The whole drill: play it start to finish, and ask the group to call each step before it comes.
  • Table top on a desk: a quick briefing for a small group — turn it to show each side of the player.
  • Spot the difference: compare the AR team with your own set-up at the touchline or in the medical room.
  • Photos and videos for your slides: a screenshot, or record the screen while the team plays (see How to use it).

The layout: the player lies on their back; Orange kneels directly behind the head, Green at the head on the player's left with the bag and AED, Red, Blue and White down the player's right, and Black — the team leader — stands on the left.
